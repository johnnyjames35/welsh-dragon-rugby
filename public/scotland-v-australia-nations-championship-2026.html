Scotland v Australia: Team News, Kick-Off Time and TV for Sunday’s Nations Championship Clash

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 15 November 2026 | ⏱ 4 min read

Scotland host Australia at Scottish Gas Murrayfield on Sunday 15 November, in Round 5 of the Nations Championship. It is the last game of the weekend, with kick-off at 3.10pm GMT. Both sides are looking to bounce back from defeats last Saturday: Scotland lost 43-21 to New Zealand, and Australia lost 45-22 to England at Twickenham.

Update: Scotland captain Sione Tuipulotu withdrew before kick-off with a knee injury, and Rory Darge took over the captaincy. See our Scotland v Australia match report.

Scotland team news

Gregor Townsend has kept the same pack and made two changes in the back three. Blair Kinghorn replaces Kyle Rowe at full-back and Darcy Graham comes in for Jamie Dobie on the left wing. Captain Sione Tuipulotu and Huw Jones stay in the centres, and Finn Russell and Ben White remain at half-back, which will be their 28th Test together. Hooker Ewan Ashman keeps his place after scoring a first-half hat-trick against the All Blacks.

Newcastle back-row Ollie Leatherbarrow is in line for a first Scotland cap off the bench. Townsend has moved to a six-two split between forwards and backs on the bench. The Scots struggled in the scrum against New Zealand, and Townsend expects another tough test at the set-piece.

Australia team news

The Wallabies have freshened things up after the England defeat. Carter Gordon returns at fly-half, Joseph-Aukuso Suaalii moves into the centres because of an injury to Isaac Henry, and Harry Potter comes in on the wing. Rob Valetini is promoted from the bench to start in the back row, with Jeremy Williams going back to lock. Brandon Paenga-Amosa and Tom Hooper are fit enough to make the replacements.

Wallabies coach Les Kiss admitted after the England game that the team is not transferring what it does in training into matches.

What is at stake

Scotland led New Zealand by only three points at half-time last Saturday before losing the second half 19-0. In July they beat Argentina and Fiji and lost to South Africa and New Zealand. Australia’s only win so far was against Italy, and they have lost to Ireland, France and England. After Argentina’s win over Italy on Saturday, the Wallabies are three points behind Argentina in the southern hemisphere table. With England and Ireland level on 20 points in the northern table, this looks like a game for pride more than a title race for both sides.

Head-to-head

Scotland won the last meeting 27-13 at Murrayfield in November 2024. Their record win over Australia is 53-24, also at Murrayfield, in November 2017.

Quick answers

What time is Scotland v Australia?

Scotland v Australia kicks off at 3.10pm GMT on Sunday 15 November 2026 at Scottish Gas Murrayfield in Edinburgh, in Round 5 of the Nations Championship.

What TV channel is Scotland v Australia on?

The match is live and free-to-air in the UK on ITV1, with STV in Scotland. Coverage starts at 2pm, and it streams on ITVX and the STV Player.

Who captains Scotland and Australia?

Sione Tuipulotu was named Scotland captain but withdrew before kick-off with a knee injury, and Rory Darge took over. Harry Wilson captains Australia.

What are the changes to the Scotland team?

Scotland make two changes: Blair Kinghorn replaces Kyle Rowe at full-back and Darcy Graham replaces Jamie Dobie on the left wing. The pack is unchanged.
