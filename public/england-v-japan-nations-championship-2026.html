England 78-15 Japan: Joseph Scores Five as England Run in 13 Tries at Twickenham

✎ By John James | 📅 14 November 2026 | ⏱ 4 min read

England beat Japan 78-15 at Allianz Stadium, Twickenham, on Saturday 14 November, in Round 5 of the Nations Championship. They ran in 13 tries for a bonus-point win, with Will Joseph scoring five of them on his first England appearance since 2022. It was England’s highest score and biggest winning margin against Japan, beating the 60-7 win at the 1987 World Cup.

Who scored

TeamScorers
England (78)Tries: Will Joseph (5), Freddie Steward, Ellis Genge, Cadan Murley, Ben Earl, George Martin, Ben Curry, Marcus Smith, Greg Fisilau. Conversions: George Ford (3), Henry Slade (2). Penalty: Ford
Japan (15)Tries: Warner Dearns, Opeti Helu. Conversion: Naoto Saito. Penalty: Saito

Half-time: England 49-10 Japan. England missed eight conversions, so the final score could have been 16 points higher.

First half: eight tries and a red card

Japan were in trouble early. Japan lock Harry Hockings was shown an early red card (a 20-minute red), and England made the most of the extra man. Joseph scored inside the first minute, then completed his hat-trick inside 20 minutes and added a fourth before the break. Saito kicked a penalty to get Japan on the board, but Steward, Genge, Murley and Earl all crossed as well, and England went in with eight tries and a 49-10 lead.

Dearns scored Japan’s first try just before half-time to cut into the deficit slightly, but it never looked like turning into a contest.

Second half: Joseph gets his fifth

Martin scored England’s ninth try early in the second half, and Helu answered for Japan shortly afterwards. Joseph then completed his five-try haul, which equalled the England record for tries in a Test, before Curry, Smith and Fisilau added the last three.

Joseph is a Gloucester winger and the brother of former England centre Jonathan Joseph.

What it means

The bonus-point win leaves England level on 20 points with Ireland in the northern hemisphere table, one point behind leaders France. Next up for England is New Zealand at Twickenham on Saturday 21 November (2.10pm), the All Blacks side that Wales beat earlier on Saturday: see our Wales v New Zealand match report. Japan travel to Murrayfield to face Scotland at the same time on the 21st.
