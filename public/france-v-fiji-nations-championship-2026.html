France 54-17 Fiji: Eight Tries as Les Bleus Take Control After a Slow Start in Lyon

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 7 November 2026 | ⏱ 3 min read

France beat Fiji 54-17 at Groupama Stadium in Lyon on Saturday, scoring eight tries to two in Round 4 of the Nations Championship. It took them about 20 minutes to get going, but they led 26-3 at half-time with the bonus point already secured, and Antoine Dupont scored with just over a minute left to take them past 50 points.

Who scored

TeamScorers
France (8 tries)Jean-Baptiste Gros, a penalty try, Sipili Falatea, Aaron Grandidier-Nkanang, Kalvin Gourgues, Théo Attissogbe, Charles Ollivon, Antoine Dupont
Fiji (2 tries)Vinaya Habosi and one other try, plus a penalty

Kalvin Gourgues and Maxime Lucu kicked France’s conversions. The match reports we checked disagree on the exact minute of several tries, so we have given the order instead.

First half: a slow start, then France take control

There was only one try in the opening 20 minutes, as Gros went over for France. Then a powerful maul was pulled down short of the line, and the referee awarded a penalty try and sent a Fiji player to the sin bin. Fiji answered with a penalty, but France scored twice more before the break through Falatea and Grandidier-Nkanang, and led 26-3 at half-time.

Second half: Dupont finishes it

Cameron Woki had a try ruled out for a knock-on early in the second half. Gourgues then extended the lead, and a move between Dupont and Louis Bielle-Biarrey set up Attissogbe. Vinaya Habosi scored Fiji’s first try, and Charles Ollivon added another late for France. Dupont scored the last try with just over a minute to go.

What it means

France host South Africa at the Stade de France on Friday 13 November, in their fifth match of the Nations Championship. Wales host New Zealand the next day: see our Wales v Japan report for how Tandy’s side got there.
