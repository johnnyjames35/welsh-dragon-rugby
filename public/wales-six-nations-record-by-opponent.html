Wales' Six Nations Record v France, Scotland, Ireland, England and Italy

✎ By John James | 📅 20 November 2026 | ⏱ 4 min read

Wales play France, Scotland, Ireland, England and Italy in the 2027 Six Nations, and the history against each one is very different. This page sets out Wales’ all-time record against each opponent, with the most recent meetings. The figures count every men’s Test between the sides, not only Six Nations games, and are correct as of 20 November 2026.

France (away, 6 February)

France lead by a single win. Their 54-12 victory in Cardiff on 15 February 2026 was their highest score in the Welsh capital, beating the 51 they scored in 1998. Wales had lost their previous seven meetings with France, so that result made it eight defeats in a row. Wales’ last win in the fixture was the 2019 World Cup quarter-final in Oita.

Scotland (away, 13 February)

Wales hold a comfortable lead, 75 wins to 54. Since 2018 the winner has taken the Doddie Weir Cup, and Scotland hold it after winning 26-23 in Cardiff in February 2026. Across the nine cup matches, Scotland have won five and Wales four.

Ireland (home, 20 February)

Wales lead 70 to 60, and seven games have been drawn. Ireland won 27-17 in Dublin in March 2026, a game Wales stayed within one score of until the last few minutes.

England (home, 6 March)

England lead 71 to 61. They have won each of the last five Six Nations meetings, including 68-14 in Cardiff in March 2025 and 48-7 at Twickenham on 7 February 2026. Wales’ last Six Nations win in the fixture was 40-24 in Cardiff on 27 February 2021, and their last win in any Test was a 20-9 warm-up win on 5 August 2023.

Italy (away, 13 March)

Wales have won 29 of 35 Tests, but Italy won the two before 2026: 24-21 in Rome in March 2024 and 22-15 in Cardiff in February 2025. Wales ended their run of 15 Six Nations defeats with a 31-17 win over Italy in Cardiff on 14 March 2026. Italy’s first win over Wales came in 2003, when they won 30-22.

Quick answers

Does Wales have a better record against Scotland or England?

Wales lead Scotland 75 wins to 54, with three draws, but trail England 61 to 71, with 12 draws.

When did Wales last beat England?

Wales last beat England in a Test on 5 August 2023, winning a World Cup warm-up 20-9 in Cardiff. Their last Six Nations win over England was 40-24 in Cardiff on 27 February 2021.

Who leads the Wales v France record?

France lead 52 wins to 51, with three draws, after their 54-12 win in Cardiff in February 2026.

How many times have Italy beaten Wales?

Italy have beaten Wales five times in 35 Tests, most recently 22-15 in Cardiff in February 2025. Wales have won 29 and one match was drawn.
