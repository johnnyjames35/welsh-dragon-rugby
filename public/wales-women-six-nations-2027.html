Wales Women's 2027 Six Nations: Fixtures, Kick-Off Times and How to Watch

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 29 October 2026 | ⏱ 5 min read

Wales Women's 2026 Six Nations was a campaign to forget: five matches, five defeats. But after a first win in 14 months in September and a win in Tokyo on 24 October, there is more hope going into 2027 than there has been for a while.

Here is the full Wales fixture list for the 2027 Women's Six Nations, with UK kick-off times, the format, how to watch, and what to expect from each round.

How the tournament works

The championship runs from 10 April to 15 May 2027, with five rounds for the six teams: England, France, Ireland, Italy, Scotland and Wales. Each team plays the other five once.

  • Super Saturdays. All three matches in each round are played back-to-back on one Saturday.
  • Points. 4 for a win and 2 for a draw. Bonus points are awarded for scoring four or more tries, and for losing by seven points or fewer.
  • A week off. There is no round between rounds three (24 April) and four (8 May).
  • Possible title decider. England v France on 15 May is billed as the possible title decider, as both sides have been dominant in recent years.

How to watch

The BBC and S4C are the UK broadcasters for the tournament. Channel and stream details for individual matches can change, so check the BBC Sport and S4C listings in the week of each game.

Where Wales are coming from

Wales lost all five matches in the 2026 Women's Six Nations, finishing a miserable campaign with a 43-24 home defeat to Italy. Their other results were Scotland 24-19 (home), France 38-7 (home), England 62-24 (away) and Ireland 33-12 (away).

This autumn has been better. Wales beat South Africa 50-19 and lost to the USA 26-24 at Cardiff Arms Park in the WXV Global Series, then beat Japan 17-0 in the first Test in Tokyo on 24 October. The second Test is at 7:00am on Saturday 31 October. For the details, see our Wales Women hub.

What to look out for

  • The opener. England, who have won eight Six Nations titles in a row, are the opponents on day one, at home. It will be a big stage and a tough start.
  • Italy away in round two. Italy won 43-24 in Cardiff last time, so this is a game Wales will be desperate to win.
  • Ireland at home in round three. Ireland won 33-12 in the 2026 meeting. This is a chance to get off the mark.
  • A late finish. Wales finish with away games at France and Scotland, in the final two rounds.

Frequently asked questions

When does the 2027 Women's Six Nations start?

The 2027 Women's Six Nations starts on Saturday 10 April 2027 and finishes on Saturday 15 May 2027. Wales open at home to England at 2:45pm.

Who do Wales Women play in the 2027 Six Nations?

Wales play England (home), Italy (away), Ireland (home), France (away) and Scotland (away), in that order, across five rounds.

How do the Super Saturdays work?

All three matches in each round are played back-to-back on the same Saturday, so fans can watch the whole round in one day. There is one week without matches, between rounds three and four.

How many points do you get in the Women's Six Nations?

A win is worth 4 points and a draw 2. Teams also earn bonus points for scoring four or more tries, and for losing by seven points or fewer.

Which channels show the Women's Six Nations in the UK?

The BBC and S4C are the UK broadcasters for the 2027 Women's Six Nations.
