Wales 35-28 New Zealand: Wales Beat the All Blacks for the First Time Since 1953

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 14 November 2026 | ⏱ 3 min read

Wales beat New Zealand 35-28 at the Principality Stadium on Saturday, their first win over the All Blacks since 19 December 1953. It ended a run of 34 straight defeats in the fixture and a wait of almost 73 years. Wales scored five tries in Round 5 of the Nations Championship, and Sam Costelow converted every one of them.

A first for this generation. Before today, Wales had beaten New Zealand only three times, in 1905, 1935 and 1953. This is the fourth win in 39 meetings.

The sides were level at 21-21 at half-time. We have left out Player of the Match and cards, because we could not yet confirm them from two sources.

First half: Wales race away, the All Blacks pull level

Wales could hardly have started better. Scrum-half Tomos Williams scored in the second minute, captain Dewi Lake crossed by the sixth minute and Costelow converted both. Cortez Ratima got New Zealand on the board, but Archie Griffin powered over for Wales’ third try inside 25 minutes and the lead went out to 21-7.

New Zealand had come in unbeaten in the Nations Championship, having won all four of their games, and they did not fade. Wallace Sititi and Fehi Fineanganofo both scored before the break, Mo’unga converted, and it was 21-21 at the interval.

Second half: James and Rees-Zammit put Wales back in front

Wales struck first after the restart. Eddie James scored in the 44th minute and Costelow converted for 28-21. Four minutes later Louis Rees-Zammit found a gap and scored, and a Costelow conversion made it 35-21. Peter Lakai scored for New Zealand soon after, and with Mo’unga’s conversion the gap was seven points.

That was the last scoring of the afternoon. The score stayed at 35-28 through the final quarter, and when French referee Pierre Brousset blew the final whistle, the Principality Stadium had the result it had waited most of a lifetime for.

What it means

A year ago New Zealand won 52-26 in Cardiff. This time Wales matched them try for try and finished with one more. It is a big result for Steve Tandy, whose side beat Japan 28-21 last Saturday, and it puts real momentum behind the final game of the autumn, against Australia at the Principality Stadium on Saturday 21 November (8:10pm). Quick answers

What was the score in Wales v New Zealand?

Wales beat New Zealand 35-28 at the Principality Stadium in Cardiff on Saturday 14 November 2026, in Round 5 of the Nations Championship. The sides were level at 21-21 at half-time.

When did Wales last beat New Zealand before this win?

Wales’ previous win over New Zealand was 13-8 at Cardiff on 19 December 1953. New Zealand had won the next 34 meetings before Wales won on 14 November 2026.

Who scored the tries in Wales v New Zealand?

Tomos Williams, Dewi Lake, Archie Griffin, Eddie James and Louis Rees-Zammit scored Wales’ five tries, and Sam Costelow converted all five. Cortez Ratima, Wallace Sititi, Fehi Fineanganofo and Peter Lakai scored New Zealand’s four, all converted by Richie Mo’unga.

When do Wales play next?

Wales host Australia at the Principality Stadium on Saturday 21 November 2026, with an 8:10pm kick-off.
