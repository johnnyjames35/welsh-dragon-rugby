Stormers 52-40 Scarlets: Roberts Hat-Trick Not Enough in a 14-Try Thriller

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 25 October 2026 | ⏱ 4 min read

The Scarlets came from 19 points down to draw level in Cape Town, but two late tries from replacement fly-half Yaqeen Ahmed gave the Stormers a 52-40 win in a 14-try United Rugby Championship thriller.

Wales centre Joe Roberts scored a hat-trick and the Scarlets took a bonus point from the game, but the Stormers have now won all six meetings between the two clubs.

Five first-half tries for the Stormers

The Scarlets were down to 14 men early on when hooker John McKee, a former Stormers player, was sin-binned for a cynical offence at the breakdown. Tighthead prop Zachary Porthen opened the scoring on 11 minutes, and Sacha Feinberg-Mngomezulu then sparked a counter-attack that ended with scrum-half Haashim Pead scoring his first try on his first start for the Stormers.

Joe Roberts hit back for the Scarlets, but loosehead Ntuthuko Mchunu crashed over as the hosts took control, and Ioan Jones replied to make it 19-14. Wing Seabelo Senatla then scored his third try in three games to secure the Stormers' bonus point, and flanker Deon Fourie finished a maul right on half-time to make it 33-14.

The Scarlets refuse to fold

With the Scarlets looking to be playing for little more than a bonus point, two quick tries after the restart changed the game. A searing break by full-back Ioan Nicholas led to a try for in-form wing Macs Page a minute into the second half. Roberts then intercepted on 45 minutes for the fourth try and the bonus point, making it 33-28.

Replacement prop Olly Reid pushed the Stormers back out to 40-28, but the Scarlets would not go away. Captain Josh Macleod finished off some neat handling to make it a seven-point game on the hour, and Roberts completed his hat-trick six minutes later. The conversion made it 40-40 with around a quarter of an hour to play.

Ahmed's late double

The Stormers bench had the final say. Ahmed went over on 73 minutes and Feinberg-Mngomezulu's conversion put the hosts back in front at 47-40. Ahmed scored again at the death to put the game to bed.

The Stormers coaches will have been anxious at times. South African reports described their play in the second half as loose and inaccurate, and the Scarlets made 21 line breaks to the Stormers' 15. Pead, on his first start, was named Player of the Match.

A young fly-half and a hard lesson

The Scarlets started 18-year-old Carwyn Leggatt-Jones at fly-half, in his first URC game of the season, and he kicked five conversions. It was a big night for a teenager against the side who beat the Scarlets 34-0 in Wales last season.

What it means for the Scarlets

The Scarlets were chasing a third win in a row after beating Benetton and then Newcastle at Parc y Scarlets, but they stay 16th in the URC. The Stormers move up to fourth, with three wins from four.

The bonus point and the comeback are something to build on. Defence is the worry, though, with eight tries conceded in Cape Town.
