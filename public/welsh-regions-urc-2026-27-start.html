Welsh Regions in the URC 2026-27: How Cardiff, Dragons, Ospreys and Scarlets Have Started

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 7 October 2026 | ⏱ 4 min read

Two rounds into the new United Rugby Championship season, the Welsh picture has split down the middle. Cardiff and Dragons have made strong starts, while Ospreys and Scarlets are both still looking for their first win. Here is how each region has got on so far, and what is coming up this weekend.

Cardiff: two from two

Cardiff began their 150th season exactly how they would have wanted. They won the opening-night derby 21-15 at Parc y Scarlets, then followed it up with a 40-27 win over Zebre Parma in their first home game of the campaign. After reaching the URC play-offs for the first time last season, Cardiff have picked up where they left off. The big test comes this weekend, away to the champions Leinster.

Dragons: unbeaten and enjoying it

After a 19-19 draw away to Benetton on the opening weekend, Dragons won a ten-try derby 38-26 against Scarlets at Rodney Parade. It was not one-way traffic. Scarlets led 14-12 at half-time, with Macs Page scoring twice, but the Dragons pack took control after the break. Elliot Dee scored two tries, with Anzelo Tuitavuki, Rodrigo Martinez, Huw Anderson and Robert Hunt also crossing.

Ospreys: a tough South African opener

Ospreys were handed the hardest possible start, with two games in South Africa. They lost 41-24 to the Sharks in Durban, then 35-19 to the Lions at Ellis Park. The Lions game was close until the break. Ospreys led 12-10 at half-time, with tries from Kieran Williams, Efan Daniel and Ross Moriarty, before the Lions pack took over in the second half. A trip back to Wales to face Dragons on Friday is their chance to get off the mark.

Scarlets: two derbies, two defeats

Scarlets have had a tough start, losing both of their opening Welsh derbies. They were beaten 21-15 at home by Cardiff, then 38-26 by Dragons in Newport. There were positives in the Rodney Parade game: they scored four tries, Macs Page got two of them, and they took a bonus point home. But after finishing 14th last season, Nigel Davies' side need wins soon, and a home game against Benetton this weekend is a good chance to get one.
