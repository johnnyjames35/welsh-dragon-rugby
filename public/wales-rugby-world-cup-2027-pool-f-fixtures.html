Wales at Rugby World Cup 2027: Pool F Fixtures, Kick-Off Times and the Route to the Knockouts

✎ By John James | 📅 27 November 2026 | ⏱ 5 min read
Wales are in Pool F with England, Tonga and Zimbabwe. The first match is against Zimbabwe at the Adelaide Oval on Saturday 2 October 2027, kicking off at 3:45am UK time.

Wales know their route through Rugby World Cup 2027. The draw put them in Pool F, and the full match schedule has since given every game a date, a venue and a kick-off time. Here is each Wales fixture, how the new 24-team format works, and where a good pool finish would take them.

Wales’s three pool fixtures

Saturday 2 OctoberWales v ZimbabweAdelaide Oval, Adelaide3:45am (12:15pm)

Saturday 16 OctoberEngland v WalesStadium Australia, Sydney9:45am (7:45pm)

All three kick-offs fall in the early hours or the morning in the UK. The other Pool F matches are England v Tonga in Brisbane on 2 October, England v Zimbabwe in Adelaide on 8 October and Tonga v Zimbabwe in Townsville on 15 October.

Who Wales are playing

  • Zimbabwe: This is the first time Wales have met Zimbabwe in a World Cup pool. Their last Test was on Wales’s summer tour in 1998, and Zimbabwe have not played at a World Cup since 1991.
  • Tonga: Wales have not faced Tonga at a World Cup since the 2003 tournament, which was also held in Australia.
  • England: The two meet at a World Cup for the first time since 2015, when England were hosts and Wales won the pool match. It is the last game of the pool, so it could decide who tops the group.

How the new format works

Rugby World Cup 2027 is the first with 24 teams, split into six pools of four. It runs from 1 October to 13 November 2027. The top two teams in every pool go through to a new round of 16, along with the four best third-placed teams.

That matters for Wales in two ways:

  • The Pool F winner plays its round-of-16 match in Perth, while the runner-up plays in Sydney.
  • Pool F’s winner is drawn against a runner-up from Pool B or D. Pool F’s runner-up plays a runner-up from Pool A or C.

The knockout rounds begin on 23 October, and the final is at Stadium Australia in Sydney on 13 November. Because the best third-placed teams also go through, a third-place finish in the pool can still be enough, which earlier 20-team World Cups did not allow.

Where Wales are heading in

Steve Tandy’s side finished last in the 2026 Six Nations, their third successive Wooden Spoon. This autumn has been more encouraging. Wales beat New Zealand 35-28 on 14 November, their first win over the All Blacks since 1953, and lost 26-29 to Australia a week later. Frequently asked questions

Who are Wales playing at Rugby World Cup 2027?

Wales are in Pool F with England, Tonga and Zimbabwe. They play Zimbabwe in Adelaide on Saturday 2 October 2027, Tonga in Melbourne on Friday 8 October and England in Sydney on Saturday 16 October.

When is the first Wales game at the 2027 Rugby World Cup?

Wales open against Zimbabwe at the Adelaide Oval on Saturday 2 October 2027. Kick-off is 12:15pm local time, which is 3:45am UK time.

How many teams go through from each pool in 2027?

The top two teams in each of the six pools go through to the round of 16, along with the four best third-placed teams.

Where is the Rugby World Cup 2027 final?

The final is on Saturday 13 November 2027 at Stadium Australia in Sydney. Wales also play England at the same ground in their last pool match.
