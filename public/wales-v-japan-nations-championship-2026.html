Wales 28-21 Japan: Adams Double and a Bonus Point, but a Nervy Finish in Cardiff

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 7 November 2026 | ⏱ 3 min read

Wales beat Japan 28-21 at the Principality Stadium on Saturday, scoring four tries to three in Round 4 of the Nations Championship. Josh Adams scored twice and Louis Rees-Zammit was named Player of the Match, but Wales lost six lineouts and had to hold on after Japan scored again with about five minutes left.

Who scored

TeamTry scorers
Wales (4 tries)Archie Griffin, Josh Adams (two), Louis Rees-Zammit
Japan (3 tries)Ben Gunter, Warner Dearns, Kenta Fukuda

Wales led 21-14 at half-time. We have not listed the conversions or the minute of each try, because the match reports we checked do not agree on them.

First half: Wales start fast, Japan keep level

Wales started brightly. Prop Archie Griffin scored inside three minutes, and Japan answered early through Ben Gunter. Adams, Rees-Zammit and Japan lock Warner Dearns all crossed before the break, and Wales went in 21-14 ahead. Rees-Zammit’s try ended a run of eight matches without a try for the winger.

Second half: Adams seals the bonus point, Japan will not go away

It was a cagey second half. Wales lost six lineouts over the afternoon, gave away needless penalties and wasted chances with handling errors. Adams scored his second try late in the half, which brought the four-try bonus point and looked to have settled it. Japan hit back through Kenta Fukuda with about five minutes left, and the finish was tense. Adams then won a penalty as the clock went red to see the game out.

What it means

It was Steve Tandy’s fourth win in 13 Tests and Wales’ second win of the Nations Championship, but the lineout and discipline will need fixing before New Zealand visit Cardiff next Saturday (14 November, 2:10pm). See our Wales v New Zealand head-to-head record and the autumn fixtures. Japan play England on the same day (4:40pm). Our Louis Rees-Zammit profile has more on today’s Player of the Match.
