Wales v Australia: Head-to-Head Record and How to Watch on 21 November

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 13 November 2026 | ⏱ 3 min read

Wales finish their autumn campaign against Australia on Saturday 21 November 2026 at the Principality Stadium. The Wallabies have the better record over more than a century of Tests, but this is a fixture with famous Welsh wins in it. Here is the head-to-head, the results that matter, and how to watch.

The record at a glance

PlayedAustralia winsWales winsDraws
4934141

The sides have met since 1908. Wales won six of the first eight meetings, but Australia have dominated the modern era, including 13 straight wins between November 2009 and November 2018.

Results that matter

DateResultVenue

The 1987 win came when Paul Thorburn landed an injury-time conversion from the touchline. The 2006 draw is still one of the highest-scoring draws in Test history. The 40-6 win in Lyon is Wales' biggest victory over Australia.

Last time out: Australia won 52-20 in Cardiff in November 2024. Wales will want to put that right on 21 November.

How to watch Wales v Australia

DateKick-offTV
Sat 21 November 20268:10pm (UK)ITV1 and STV (English), S4C (Welsh)

ITV and STV have free-to-air rights to every Nations Championship game, and S4C is live on all of Wales' matches, with streaming on ITVX, S4C Clic and BBC iPlayer. The match is the last of Wales' three November home games, after New Zealand on 14 November.
