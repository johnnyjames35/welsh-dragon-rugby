Privacy Policy

How Welsh Dragon Rugby collects, uses and protects your information.

Last updated: 14 August 2026

Welsh Dragon Rugby is an independent fan content site. We do not require you to create an account or submit personal details to read the site, and we do not sell your personal data.

1. Who We Are

Welsh Dragon Rugby ("we", "us", "our") is operated by Big Bulldog UK Ltd, a company based in South Wales, United Kingdom. This policy explains how we handle information when you visit welshdragonrugby.co.uk.

2. Information We Collect

Because the site does not require sign-up or accounts, we do not collect names, email addresses or payment details from visitors browsing or reading the site.

We do automatically collect standard technical data through cookies and similar technologies when you visit, including:

  • IP address and approximate location
  • Browser type and device information
  • Pages visited, time on site and referring pages

If you contact us directly (for example via the contact page or email), we will also hold the details you choose to share with us, such as your name and email address, solely to respond to your message.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the technical data described above to:

  • Understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it
  • Keep the site secure and working correctly
  • Serve relevant advertising through Google AdSense (see below)

Any information you send us directly is used only to respond to your enquiry.

4. Cookies and Advertising

We use Google Analytics to understand site traffic, and may display adverts served by Google AdSense. Both may set cookies on your device. Full details of the cookies we use, and how to control them, are set out in our Cookie Policy.

5. Who We Share Your Information With

We do not sell your personal data. Technical and analytics data may be processed by our service providers, currently Google (Analytics and AdSense) and API-Sports (our live sports data provider), solely to operate and improve the site.

6. How Long We Keep Data

Analytics data is retained according to Google's standard retention settings. Messages sent to us directly are kept only as long as needed to deal with your enquiry, unless we are required to keep them longer for legal reasons.

7. Your Rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Ask us to delete your data
  • Object to how we use your data
  • Withdraw your consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect any information we hold from loss, misuse and unauthorised access. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page will always show when it was last updated.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us here.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk or by calling 0303 123 1113.
