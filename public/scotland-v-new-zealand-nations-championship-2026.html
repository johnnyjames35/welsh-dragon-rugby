New Zealand 43-21 Scotland: Ashman Hat-Trick Not Enough at Murrayfield

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 7 November 2026 | ⏱ 3 min read

New Zealand beat Scotland 43-21 at Murrayfield on Saturday, scoring seven tries to three in Round 4 of the Nations Championship. Scotland hooker Ewan Ashman scored a hat-trick, but the All Blacks pulled clear after the break and Scotland did not score in the second half.

Finn Russell converted all three Scotland tries. Damian McKenzie kicked four conversions for New Zealand; the tries by Sititi, Lakai and Moorby went unconverted.

First half: Ashman keeps Scotland in it

New Zealand scored first through back-rower Wallace Sititi after three minutes, and Ashman replied four minutes later. De Groot and Lakai then gave the All Blacks a 17-7 lead by the 19th minute. Ashman scored again on 25 minutes, Beauden Barrett crossed on 30, and Ashman completed his hat-trick just before half-time. It was 24-21 to New Zealand at the break.

Second half: New Zealand pull away

Josh Moorby scored on 48 minutes and Beauden Barrett added his second two minutes later to put New Zealand 36-21 up. Scotland never found a way back, and Asafo Aumua's try on 67 minutes made it 43-21. New Zealand scored 19 points after half-time to none for Scotland.

What it means

Scotland came into the match still seeking a first win over New Zealand, and that wait goes on.
