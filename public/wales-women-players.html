Wales Women Players: Star Profiles

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 7 November 2026 | ⏱ 2 min read

Meet the players

Wales Women finished 2026 with three wins from four in the WXV Global Series. These are four of the players behind the turnaround, and the names to know before the 2027 Six Nations. We will add more profiles over the coming months.

Player profiles

Sisilia Tuipulotu
Prop · Harlequins
Try-scoring prop from a famous rugby family, with four WXV tries this autumn and her eyes on the 2027 Lions tour.
Alex Callender
Flanker · Harlequins · Co-captain
Former Wales under-21 netballer who won her 50th cap in Tokyo this autumn.
Kate Williams
Flanker · Gloucester-Hartpury · Co-captain
Swansea-born leader who captained Wales in the 2026 Six Nations.
Jasmine Joyce-Butchers
Wing · three-time Olympian
The first British sevens player at three Olympics, now playing her club rugby back home in Wales.

Quick answers

Who are the Wales Women co-captains?

Alex Callender and Kate Williams co-captained Wales in the 2026 WXV Global Series.

Who is the Wales Women head coach?

Sean Lynn is head coach of Wales Women.
