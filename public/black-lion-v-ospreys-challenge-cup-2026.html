Black Lion 14-39 Ospreys: Seven Tries and a Bonus Point in Tbilisi

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 17 October 2026 | ⏱ 4 min read

Ospreys made the long trip to Georgia and came home with maximum points. Mark Jones' side beat Black Lion 39-14 in Tbilisi on Saturday, scoring seven tries to two to open their 2026-27 Challenge Cup campaign with a bonus-point win.

Jones made big changes, with six players making their first starts of the season. They responded with a professional, controlled performance against a side that has caused upsets in Europe before.

How the game went

The back row does the damage

Ospreys scored twice in the first 12 minutes, through flanker Gwilym Evans and number eight Morgan Morse. Black Lion had a brief moment of hope when scrum-half Giorgi Spanderashvili scored around the 20-minute mark, but Ospreys pulled away again.

Flanker Huw Sutton made it three tries for the back row. Then, five minutes before the break, wing Luke Morgan scored after hooker Lewis Lloyd broke a tackle from a line-out and centre Lalakai Foketi gave the final pass. That made it 22-7 at half-time, with the bonus point already in reach.

Three more after the break

Luke Morgan dived over in the corner for his second try. Hooker Lewis Lloyd then finished a fine team move out wide after a pass from scrum-half Reuben Morgan-Williams, and prop Kian Hire added the seventh. Black Lion had the last word, with a late consolation try.

The one weak spot: goal-kicking

The scoreline could have been bigger. Fly-half Lawson Creighton converted only two of the seven tries. On a day when the result was never really in doubt, it did not matter. In tighter games later in the pool, those missed kicks could cost points.

Why this was a bonus-point win

New bonus-point rule. In this season's Challenge Cup, a team only gets a try bonus point if it scores at least three more tries than the opposition. Scoring four tries is no longer enough on its own.

Ospreys scored seven tries to Black Lion's two, five more, so they took the full five points from the match.

Why the win matters

Black Lion are not a side to take lightly at home. The Georgian team have beaten established clubs before, including a famous win over Scarlets in 2023. With so many changes, this could have been a banana skin for Ospreys. Instead, it was a comfortable afternoon.

It also caps a demanding start to the season. Ospreys have already played two URC matches in South Africa, and this trip to Georgia added more air miles.

What comes next for Ospreys

Ospreys now get some time at home. They host Dragons and then Cardiff in back-to-back Welsh derbies in the URC. Ospreys led the Welsh regions last season by winning the Welsh Shield, and two home derby wins would put them in a strong position again.
