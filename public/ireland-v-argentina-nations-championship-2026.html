Ireland 42-12 Argentina: Hugo Keenan Hat-Trick in Dublin

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 7 November 2026 | ⏱ 3 min read

Ireland beat Argentina 42-12 at the Aviva Stadium on Friday night, scoring six tries to two in Round 4 of the Nations Championship. Full-back Hugo Keenan scored a hat-trick and was named player of the match, and Ireland took a bonus-point win to open their November window.

Every Ireland try was converted: Sam Prendergast kicked five and Ciaran Frawley added the last after Kelleher finished from a driving maul. Minute timings vary slightly between live reports.

First half: Argentina strike first

Argentina struck first when wing Mateo Carreras scored after ten minutes. Ireland replied through Keenan, then captain Dan Sheehan, and Keenan crossed again before the half hour. Ireland led 21-5 at the break.

Second half: Ireland pull clear

Fly-half Prendergast scored his first international try straight after the restart. Carreras grabbed his second to give the Pumas some hope, but Keenan completed his hat-trick and Kelleher's try put the game beyond reach by the hour mark.

Argentina's evening was not helped by yellow cards for Justo Piccardo and Efrain Elias, or by an early head injury to captain Julian Montoya. Ireland finished with 58% of territory, 138 carries to 113 and 463 metres gained to 373.

What it means

It was Ireland's third win from four Nations Championship games, after summer wins over Australia and Japan and a defeat to New Zealand. Argentina have now lost all 12 of their meetings with Ireland in Dublin, although they beat the British and Irish Lions at the same ground in 2025.

For Welsh fans, this was a reminder of the Argentina side that beat Wales 35-21 in July. Wales open their own autumn today against Japan at the Principality Stadium (4:40pm): see our match preview and how to watch.

Quick answers

What was the score in Ireland v Argentina?

Ireland beat Argentina 42-12 at the Aviva Stadium in Dublin on Friday 6 November 2026, in Round 4 of the Nations Championship. Ireland led 21-5 at half-time.

Who scored Ireland's tries against Argentina?

Hugo Keenan scored a hat-trick, with further tries from captain Dan Sheehan, Sam Prendergast and Ronan Kelleher. Prendergast kicked five conversions and Ciaran Frawley one.

Who scored Argentina's tries?

Mateo Carreras scored both of Argentina's tries.
