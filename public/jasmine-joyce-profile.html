Jasmine Joyce-Butchers: Age, Club, Olympics and Wales Career

One of Wales' best-known players

Jasmine Joyce-Butchers, often known as Jaz, is one of the best-known players in Welsh women's rugby. Known for her pace, she became the first British rugby sevens player to compete at three Olympic Games.

Bristol Bears and the move home

She joined Bristol Bears in 2020 and spent six years there, helping the club to two Premiership Women's Rugby semi-finals. In summer 2026 she and her wife, fellow Wales international Alisha Joyce, left Bristol to play their club rugby in Wales, so they could spend more time with their son Ralphie, born at the end of 2025. Both have already played for Brython Thunder in the Celtic Challenge.

Where she stands with Wales

Joyce-Butchers played in the 2026 Women's Six Nations, but she was not picked in the 30-player squad that toured Japan this autumn. With 57 caps and her experience, she will be one of the names to watch when Sean Lynn picks his squad for the 2027 Six Nations.

Quick answers

How old is Jasmine Joyce?

Jasmine Joyce-Butchers was born on 9 October 1995, so she is 31.

How many Olympics has Jasmine Joyce been to?

Three: Rio 2016, Tokyo 2020 and Paris 2024, all with Team GB sevens. She was the first British rugby sevens player to compete at three Olympics.

Who is Jasmine Joyce married to?

She is married to Wales back-rower Alisha Joyce. The couple have a son, Ralphie.

Why was Jasmine Joyce not in the Wales squad for Japan?

Sean Lynn did not include her in the 30-player squad for the two Tests in Japan in October 2026. No specific reason was given in the reports we checked.
