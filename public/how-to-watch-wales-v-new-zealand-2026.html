How to Watch Wales v New Zealand: TV Channel, Live Stream and Free Options

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 13 November 2026 | ⏱ 2 min read

Wales v New Zealand kicks off at 2:10pm UK time on Saturday 14 November 2026 at the Principality Stadium in Cardiff. It is free to watch in the UK: ITV has the English-language coverage and S4C has the Welsh-language coverage.

Where to watch Wales v New Zealand

Is Wales v New Zealand free to watch?

Yes. ITV and STV hold the UK free-to-air rights to every game in the 2026 Nations Championship, so you do not need a sports subscription or a pay-per-view purchase. S4C has live rights to all of Wales' Nations Championship matches too, and its coverage is on S4C, S4C Clic and BBC iPlayer.

Kick-off time

The game starts at 2:10pm in the UK (GMT). It is Round 5 of the Nations Championship and the second of Wales' three home games this autumn, after the 28-21 win over Japan on 7 November. For the side Steve Tandy has picked, read our Wales team announcement, and for the history behind the fixture see the Wales v New Zealand head-to-head record.

Watching from outside the UK? This guide covers UK viewing only. Broadcast rights overseas differ by country and we have not confirmed them, so check your local Nations Championship listings.

What happens next?

Wales finish their autumn at home to Australia on Saturday 21 November, with kick-off at 8:10pm. The same free-to-air options apply. All three home fixtures are in our autumn fixtures guide.
