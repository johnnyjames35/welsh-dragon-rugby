Wales 2027 Six Nations: Fixtures, Kick-Off Times and How to Watch

✎ By John James | 📅 20 November 2026 | ⏱ 4 min read

Wales open the 2027 Guinness Men’s Six Nations away to France on Saturday 6 February, a day after Ireland host England in the championship opener on Friday 5 February. Wales then have two home games in Cardiff, against Ireland and England, and finish in Rome on Super Saturday, 13 March. Here are the fixtures, kick-off times and how to watch.

All kick-off times are UK time (GMT). There is one fallow week in the championship, after Round 3, which is why Wales play Ireland on 20 February and England on 6 March. See our Six Nations explained guide for how the points, bonus points and trophies work.

How Wales finished in 2026

Wales ended the 2026 Six Nations in sixth place on six points. They won their final game, beating Italy 31-17 in Cardiff on 14 March, their first Six Nations win since 2023. France won the title on 21 points after a 48-46 win over England on the last day, with Ireland second on 19, Scotland third on 16, Italy fourth on nine and England fifth on eight.

Where Wales are now

Steve Tandy is head coach. Wales beat Japan 28-21 and then New Zealand 35-28 in November, their first win over the All Blacks since 1953, in the autumn Nations Championship. Read the full story of the win over New Zealand and our autumn fixtures guide. The Six Nations squad has not been named yet, and we will add it here once it has.

How to watch the 2027 Six Nations in the UK

The BBC and ITV share the men’s Six Nations in the UK on free-to-air television, under a deal that runs until at least 2029. S4C also shows Wales games with Welsh-language commentary. You need a valid TV Licence to watch live TV or stream live on BBC iPlayer and ITVX.

Which channel will show each Wales game has not been confirmed in the sources we checked, so look at the official broadcaster listing nearer the time. We will add the channel for each round to this page as soon as it is announced.

Tickets, travel and your calendar

When the Welsh Rugby Union announced the fixtures, tickets for the home games against Ireland and England had not yet gone on sale, and supporters could register interest through the WRU. Travel packages for the away games were being offered through Gullivers Sports Travel, the WRU’s official supporter travel partner. Check wru.wales for the current position. The WRU also publishes a Welsh Rugby Calendar that fans can sync to their own phone calendar.

Prefer a quick download? Add the five Wales matches to your calendar with our Wales 2027 Six Nations calendar file. The end times in it are estimates, and we will update it if any kick-off time changes.

Trophies Wales can win

Wales play for the Doddie Weir Cup when they face Scotland in Round 2. Because Wales also play Ireland and England in 2027, they can win the Triple Crown by beating all three of the other home nations.

Looking for the women’s tournament? The Women’s Six Nations starts on 10 April, and Wales open at home to England. See our Wales Women 2027 Six Nations guide.

Quick answers

When does Wales' 2027 Six Nations start?

Wales play France away at the Stade de France on Saturday 6 February 2027, with kick-off at 4:40pm UK time. The championship starts a day earlier with Ireland v England on Friday 5 February.

Who do Wales play in the 2027 Six Nations?

Wales play France (away), Scotland (away), Ireland (home), England (home) and Italy (away), in that order.

Where can I watch the 2027 Six Nations in the UK?

The BBC and ITV share the tournament on free-to-air TV, and S4C shows Wales matches in Welsh. The channel for each Wales match has not been confirmed in the sources we checked.

When is the last round of the 2027 Six Nations?

Round 5 is Super Saturday on 13 March 2027, with Italy v Wales at 2:10pm UK time, followed by England v Scotland and Ireland v France.
