Wales Women Head to Japan: Squad, Dates and Kick-Off Times for the Two Tests

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 16 October 2026 | ⏱ 5 min read

Wales Women's autumn ends on the other side of the world. After an eight-try win over South Africa and a two-point defeat to the USA at Cardiff Arms Park, Sean Lynn's side head to Japan for two Tests in the WXV Global Series.

Here is everything Welsh fans need: the dates and UK kick-off times, the 30-player squad, how to watch, and why these two games matter.

Japan v Wales: dates and kick-off times

DateUK kick-offMatchVenue
Sat 24 October10:30amJapan v WalesPrince Chichibu Memorial Stadium, Tokyo
Sat 31 October7:00amJapan v WalesKumagaya Rugby Stadium, Kumagaya
Watch out for the clocks. The UK clocks go back on Sunday 25 October, between the two Tests. So set your alarm carefully for the second game: it is a 7am start in the UK.

For coverage in the UK, the games are listed on BBC iPlayer. Channel details can change, so check the BBC Sport listings in the week of each match.

The big talking points

Nel Metcalfe is back

The Gloucester Hartpury full-back missed the 2026 Women's Six Nations through injury and has not played for Wales since the defeat to Fiji at the 2025 World Cup. Her return adds another attacking option to the back three.

Two uncapped props

Stella Orrin of Brython Thunder and Crystal James of Trailfinders Women are both in line for their first caps. They join Amy Williams, Elan Jones and Shanelle Williams, who all made their Wales debuts in the 50-19 win over South Africa in September.

Who misses out

There is no place for Jaz Joyce or Alisha Joyce, and wing Seren Singleton, who played in the win over South Africa, is also left out.

What Sean Lynn said

Lynn called it a tough selection after the South Africa and USA games. He said the team had asked the players to "move the dial", and believes they did, pointing to the eight tries against South Africa and the players' disappointment at losing narrowly to a USA side ranked sixth in the world. He also admitted Wales still have a long way to go, but described the tour as an exciting challenge for everyone involved.

How the two sides compare

On paper, this is about as even as international rugby gets. When the squad was named on 5 October, Wales were 11th in the world rankings and Japan were 12th, just one place behind.

Japan have had a tough run. They lost to Italy and Ireland in the WXV Global Series, then lost 43-19 to Ireland in an extra Test in Cork. Wales, meanwhile, have the momentum of that South Africa win and the belief that came from fighting back against the USA.

The last time the two teams met, Wales won 19-10 in WXV2 in Cape Town in October 2024. Beating Japan twice on their own patch would be a very different challenge, but it is a realistic target, and two wins would make this a genuinely memorable autumn for Wales.

Why it matters

Wales finished bottom of the 2026 Women's Six Nations without a win. This autumn has been the first real sign of a turnaround under Lynn. Coming home from Japan with results would give the squad something solid to build on before the next Six Nations.
