Scarlets 47-14 Newcastle: Seven Tries and a Bonus Point at Parc y Scarlets

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 17 October 2026 | ⏱ 4 min read

Scarlets made it two wins in a row with a dominant 47-14 victory over Newcastle Red Bulls at Parc y Scarlets on Saturday. They led 26-0 at half-time, scored seven tries in total, and took a bonus point to start their 2026-27 Challenge Cup campaign in style.

Winger Macs Page marked his 50th appearance for the region with two tries.

How the game went

Four first-half tries

Fly-half Sam Costelow opened the scoring in the 12th minute and converted his own try. Number eight Fletcher Anderson powered over nine minutes later. Centre Johnny Williams, who used to play for Newcastle, scored the third against his old club, and scrum-half Gareth Davies added the fourth just before half-time.

Anderson and Davies had both been promoted to the starting team after making a big impact off the bench in the previous week's win over Benetton. They repaid the faith with a try each.

Scarlets keep their grip

Scarlets had a fifth try ruled out for a forward pass two minutes into the second half. The video referee also turned down a Newcastle try claim from Obi Ene, and the visitors then lost Murray McCallum to the sin bin.

Scarlets made the extra man count. Wing Ellis Mee scored, and Page added two more. Newcastle finished with spirit, with late tries from Oli Spencer and Ollie Leatherbarrow, but the result had long been settled.

Why this was a bonus-point win

New bonus-point rule. In this season's Challenge Cup, a team only gets a try bonus point if it scores at least three more tries than the opposition. Scoring four tries is no longer enough on its own.

Scarlets scored seven tries to Newcastle's two, five more, so they took the full five points.

A win over a familiar face

There was extra interest in Llanelli because former Scarlets and Wales fly-half Stephen Jones is now on Newcastle's coaching staff. It was not a happy return for him. Scarlets were in control from the 12th minute onwards.

Momentum building

Last season was a hard one for Scarlets, who finished 14th of 16 in the URC. This makes it two wins in a row, following the 27-26 comeback win over Benetton, sealed by a late Costelow penalty. Ellis Mee and Blair Murray, both featured on our young Wales players to watch page, were in the starting XV.

What comes next for Scarlets

The Challenge Cup now takes a break, and Scarlets head to South Africa for URC games. They will travel with confidence after two wins in a row and a seven-try performance.
