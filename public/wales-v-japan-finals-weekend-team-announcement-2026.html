Wales Team to Face Japan at Twickenham: Jenkins Captains, Morgan-Williams Makes First Start

✎ By John James | 📅 26 November 2026 | ⏱ 3 min read

Wales have named their matchday 23 for Saturday's Nations Championship game against Japan at Twickenham, with lock Dafydd Jenkins captaining the side. Head coach Steve Tandy has made five changes to the starting XV that lost 26-29 to Australia in Cardiff last Saturday. Kick-off at Allianz Stadium is at 1:10pm on Saturday 28 November.

The five changes

Compared with the side that started against Australia, these are the five changes to the starting XV:

  • Rhys Carré starts at loosehead prop, in place of Nicky Smith. It is his first start of this November window.
  • Ryan Elias starts at hooker, in place of Dewi Lake.
  • Adam Beard returns in the second row alongside Jenkins, in place of Ben Carter, who is not in the 23.
  • James Botham comes back into the back row at number seven, in place of Jac Morgan. Tandy has said Botham was outstanding against the All Blacks.
  • Reuben Morgan-Williams makes his first Wales start at scrum-half, in place of Tomos Williams.

Jenkins takes over the captaincy for the match. Lake, Jac Morgan and Tomos Williams are all on the bench.

Bench news

Lake, Jac Morgan, Tomos Williams and winger Ellis Mee come into the replacements. Ryan Elias and Reuben Morgan-Williams move up to the starting XV, while Kane James and Louie Hennessey are not in the 23. Gareth Thomas, Dillon Lewis, Taine Plumtree and Joe Hawkins keep their places on the bench.

What is at stake

This is the Nations Championship play-off between the fifth-placed teams in the Northern and Southern Hemisphere tables, which decides ninth and tenth overall. Wales finished fifth in the north after the Australia defeat. For the build-up, read our Wales v Japan finals weekend preview.

Japan's team had not been confirmed when this article was published, so we have not listed it. We will add it once it is announced.
