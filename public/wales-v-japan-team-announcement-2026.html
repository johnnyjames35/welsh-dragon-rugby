Wales Team to Face Japan: Lake Captains a New-Look Midfield in Cardiff

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 5 November 2026 | ⏱ 3 min read

Wales have named their matchday 23 for Saturday's Nations Championship Round 4 game against Japan, with hooker Dewi Lake captaining the side. The match is at the Principality Stadium in Cardiff and kicks off at 4:40pm on Saturday 7 November, live on ITV and S4C.

It is Wales' first home game of the inaugural Nations Championship, and the first of three Saturdays in Cardiff this month, with New Zealand and Australia to follow.

A new-look midfield

The biggest talking point is in the centres, where Max Llewellyn starts at 12 and Eddie James at 13. Before the team was named, reports had them replacing the Ben Thomas and Joe Hawkins pairing used earlier. Steve Tandy has said he sees James as "more as a 13" who can also cover 12.

Sam Costelow wears 10 and Tomos Williams partners him at scrum-half. Dan Edwards, who held the fly-half shirt for much of last season, is among the replacements. Blair Murray starts at full-back, with Louis Rees-Zammit and Josh Adams on the wings.

What Tandy said

Head coach Steve Tandy pointed to the depth of the squad, saying “lots of competition for position within the squad” is where Wales want to be. He said the team are excited to be back at home and want to start the next phase of their Nations Championship campaign “strongly”.

Pack and bench

Nicky Smith, Lake and Archie Griffin make up the front row. Dafydd Jenkins and Ben Carter are in the second row, and the back row is Alex Mann at 6, Jac Morgan at 7 and Aaron Wainwright at 8.

The bench has five forwards and three backs. Ryan Elias, Rhys Carre and Tomas Francis cover the front row, Teddy Williams is the second-row replacement and James Botham the back-row option. Reuben Morgan-Williams, Edwards and Louie Hennessey are the backs.

Rio Dyer is out injured and his replacement in the squad, Macs Page, is not in the 23. Read the full story in our report on the Dyer and Page change.
