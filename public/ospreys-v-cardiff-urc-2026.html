Ospreys 0-26 Cardiff: Bowen Double and Bonus Point in the St Helen’s Derby

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 1 November 2026 | ⏱ 2 min read

Cardiff Rugby shut out the Ospreys 26-0 at St Helen’s on Saturday 31 October in a one-sided Welsh derby, with Tom Bowen scoring twice and the visitors leading 19-0 at half-time.

The result

Cardiff Rugby26 WINTries: Tom Bowen 2, Josh Adams, Tom Howe. Conversions: Jarrod Evans 2, Callum Sheedy 1

Half-time: Ospreys 0-19 Cardiff. Venue: St Helen’s, Swansea. Competition: United Rugby Championship, Round 5.

Cardiff short-handed but ruthless

Cardiff arrived badly depleted. Their own match report says they were without 18 players because of international call-ups and injuries, which made the scoreline more striking.

Bowen scored twice in the first half, the first around the 16th minute, and Josh Adams added a third try about half an hour in. Jarrod Evans converted Bowen’s two tries and missed the kick after Adams’s score.

No way back for the Ospreys

The Ospreys never found a way back. One report says Steffan Thomas appeared to score, but the TMO ruled it out after Johan Mulder stopped the grounding. Another says that in the closing seconds Steffan Emanuel denied Keelan Giles on the line, so the Ospreys finished without a point.

Cardiff sealed the bonus point in the second half when replacement hooker Tom Howe scored from a driving maul, after the visitors won a turnover penalty. That was the fourth try and took them to 26 points.

What next?

The result closes a block of matches before the November international window. We will add reaction here when the clubs publish it.

Four tries for Cardiff. Nothing for the Ospreys.
A derby night in Swansea to forget for the home side.
