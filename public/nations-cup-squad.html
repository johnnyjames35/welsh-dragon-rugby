👑 Captain: Dewi Lake

Steve Tandy has kept Dewi Lake as Wales captain for the November Nations Championship, with the hooker now playing his club rugby at Gloucester alongside fellow squad member Jac Morgan. Wales host Japan (7 Nov), New Zealand (14 Nov) and Australia (21 Nov) on three consecutive Saturdays, all at the Principality Stadium.
