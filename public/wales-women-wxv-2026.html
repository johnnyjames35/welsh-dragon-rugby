Wales Women in the WXV Global Series 2026: Results So Far and the Japan Tour

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 3 October 2026 | ⏱ 5 min read

After a long, hard run without a win, Wales Women have finally given their supporters something to shout about. Two home matches at Cardiff Arms Park in the new WXV Global Series brought a thumping win over South Africa and an agonising two-point defeat to the USA. Next comes the toughest kind of trip: two Tests in Japan.

Here is how the autumn has gone so far, and what to look out for before Sean Lynn's side fly out.

Wales 50-19 South Africa: the night it clicked

This was the performance Welsh fans had been waiting for. Wales scored eight tries and led 38-5 at half-time, ending a run of nine Test defeats and claiming their first Test win in 14 months.

Prop Sisilia Tuipulotu scored twice, with further tries from Courtney Keight, Seren Lockwood, Maisie Davies, Kelsey Jones, Lisa Neumann and Alex Callender. Freya Bell kicked five conversions. One of the big talking points before kick-off was Lynn's decision to start Keira Bevan at fly-half for the first time for Wales, and it paid off. There were also international debuts off the bench for Shanelle Williams and Elan Jones.

After the game Lynn said the staff had held a big meeting in July to reset how the team works. On this evidence, it has made a real difference.

Wales 24-26 USA: so close

A week later, the USA came to the Arms Park and made Wales work for every inch. The visitors raced into a 14-0 lead and were 21-5 up after Keira Bevan had scored Wales' first try.

At that point plenty of teams would have folded. Wales did not. They hit back to lead 24-21 at half-time, a superb turnaround. But USA number eight Freda Tafuna was the difference. She completed her hat-trick in the second half to put the Eagles back in front, and Wales could not find the score to win it.

A defeat, yes. But fighting back from 16 points down against a side ranked above them shows a team with real belief.

Why it matters. Wales finished bottom of the 2026 Women's Six Nations, losing all five games, and went out of the 2025 World Cup at the pool stage. One win and a narrow loss is not a turnaround on its own, but it is the most encouraging stretch this team has had in a long time.

Next up: two Tests in Japan

Wales finish their WXV Global Series campaign with a two-Test series away to Japan at the end of October.

Different sources currently disagree on the exact day of the first Test, so we will update this page as soon as the WRU confirms the date and kick-off times. With Japan eight or nine hours ahead of the UK, expect early starts for anyone watching at home.

Japan are ranked a couple of places above Wales in the world rankings, so these are genuinely competitive Tests. The last time the two sides met, Wales won 19-10 in WXV2 in Cape Town in October 2024.

How to watch and follow

Broadcast details for the Japan Tests have not been confirmed yet. Keep an eye on the WRU website and BBC Sport Wales closer to the time. We will add TV and streaming information here as soon as it is announced.

Why now is a great time to follow Wales Women

If you have been meaning to get behind the women's team, this is the moment. The style of rugby against South Africa was fast and exciting, the USA comeback showed real spirit, and Lynn has said he wants more people to come and see what this team is about. Every bit of support counts.
