Nations Championship Round 6: England Beat All Blacks, Ireland Stun South Africa, France Reach the Final

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 22 November 2026 | ⏱ 6 min read

Wales lost 29-26 to Australia in Cardiff (read our Wales v Australia match report), but there were five other games in Round 6 of the Nations Championship on Saturday 21 November. England beat New Zealand by 25 points, Ireland beat South Africa, Scotland ran in 11 tries against Japan, Fiji won for the first time in the competition and France secured the bonus point they needed to reach the final. France and South Africa will now meet at Twickenham on Sunday 29 November.

England 37-12 New Zealand

England beat the All Blacks at Twickenham by 25 points, a performance Sky Sports called the most complete under Steve Borthwick and a record win over New Zealand. They scored five tries: Henry Slade (5th minute, reaching a Fin Smith grubber), Jack van Poortvliet (32nd), Ben Earl (36th), Ollie Chessum (43rd) and Seb Atkinson (63rd). Fin Smith kicked two conversions and Marcus Smith one conversion and two penalties.

New Zealand scored through Ethan de Groot (39th) and Beauden Barrett (52nd), with Damian McKenzie converting the second. England led 19-5 at half-time. Earl’s try came from a scrum after the referee did not award a turnover penalty following a Fletcher Newell knock-on, and Chessum’s score survived a TMO check for a possible double movement. England and New Zealand have a rematch at Twickenham on Saturday 28 November, because France’s bonus point left England second in the northern table.

Ireland 20-17 South Africa

Ireland beat the world champions in Dublin, and Robert Baloucoune scored two first-half tries on the way to a man-of-the-match performance. Ronan Kelleher scored just before half-time to give Ireland a 17-10 lead at the break. Morne van den Berg and Malcolm Marx scored South Africa’s tries, and Sacha Feinberg-Mngomezulu kicked an early penalty.

The Springboks’ scrum was the story of the first half. It set up van den Berg’s try to level the scores against the run of play, four minutes before the break, although the biggest roar of the game came when Ireland’s pack knocked the South African scrum backwards, which was a rare sight. Ireland held on in a tense finish, with a turnover late on and a Springbok spill at the final whistle. Sky Sports noted it was South Africa’s first back-to-back defeat since 2022.

The losing bonus point was still enough to put South Africa in the final. They played without head coach Rassie Erasmus, who is serving a six-match World Rugby ban, and the South African coaching staff have denied he had any contact with the team during the game. Ireland now play Australia at Twickenham on Friday 27 November (8.10pm).

France 45-17 Argentina

France scored seven tries to take the attacking bonus point they needed, finishing top of the northern table and booking their place in the final. It was not smooth early on. Returning fly-half Matthieu Jalibert dropped Argentina’s first high ball, France were error-prone, and Peato Mauvaka only scored from a lineout catch-and-drive in the 34th minute. Argentina were level at one stage after Justo Piccardo burrowed over following a succession of scrums.

Argentina stayed in the game until two second-half cards cost them dearly, one of them for replacement prop Mayco Vivas, who was sent to the sin-bin for head contact in a card that was upgraded to a red after a bunker review. Centre Kalvin Gourgues scored two tries, the first set up by Jalibert shortly after the break. France coach Fabien Galthié said he was very happy to be in the final and that South Africa would be ready for it. France beat the Springboks 21-19 in Paris last week; read our report on that game.

Scotland 67-5 Japan

Scotland scored 11 tries at Murrayfield, and wing Darcy Graham and forward Liam McConnell both scored hat-tricks. Japan’s only reply was a single try. Gregor Townsend fielded a much-changed side, and said his fringe players had staked a claim for World Cup involvement next year.

The win gave Scotland four wins from six and fourth place in the northern table, rather than the third place they had hoped to take from Ireland. They have injury concerns going into Finals Weekend, with Matt Fagerson hurt, and they play Argentina at Twickenham on Sunday 29 November (1.10pm).

Italy 16-21 Fiji

Fiji won their first game of the Nations Championship, coming from 13-7 down at half-time in Udine. Captain and hooker Tevita Ikanivere scored two tries, the second five minutes after the break, and flanker Kitione Salawa added a third eight minutes later and was named Player of the Match. Italy had led through a Lorenzo Cannone try and the boot of full-back Tommaso Allan, but their only second-half points were Allan’s third penalty of the match, which came more than 30 minutes after their previous score. Fiji held on thanks in part to 17 turnovers in the game.

It was Italy’s sixth defeat in six games, so they finish as the only team without a win, and their seventh loss in a row overall. The sides meet again at Twickenham on Friday 27 November (4.40pm) in the game between the bottom-placed teams from each table.

Quick answers

What were the Nations Championship results on Saturday 21 November 2026?

England 37-12 New Zealand, Scotland 67-5 Japan, Ireland 20-17 South Africa, Italy 16-21 Fiji, France 45-17 Argentina and Wales 26-29 Australia.

Who is in the Nations Championship final?

France play South Africa in the final at Allianz Stadium, Twickenham, on Sunday 29 November 2026 (4.40pm UK time). France finished top of the northern table and South Africa top of the southern table.

How did South Africa reach the final after losing to Ireland?

South Africa lost 20-17 in Dublin but picked up a losing bonus point, which was enough to keep them top of the southern hemisphere table.

Why did England miss the Nations Championship final?

England beat New Zealand 37-12 but needed France to fail to win with a bonus point against Argentina. France won 45-17 with seven tries, took the bonus point and finished top of the northern table, leaving England second.

Who do Wales play on Finals Weekend?

Wales play Japan at Allianz Stadium, Twickenham, on Saturday 28 November 2026 at 1.10pm UK time.
