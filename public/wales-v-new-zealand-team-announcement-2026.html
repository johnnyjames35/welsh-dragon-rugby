Wales Team to Face New Zealand: Four Changes as Basham Returns and Adams Is Dropped

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 12 November 2026 | ⏱ 4 min read

Wales have named their matchday 23 for Saturday's Nations Championship game against New Zealand, with hooker Dewi Lake captaining the side. Head coach Steve Tandy has made four changes to the starting XV from the team that beat Japan 28-21 last Saturday. The match is at the Principality Stadium in Cardiff and kicks off at 2:10pm on Saturday 14 November, live on ITV.

The four changes

Compared with the side named for Japan, these are the four changes to the starting XV:

  • Taine Basham starts at number eight, in place of Aaron Wainwright, who is out with a minor calf injury. It is Basham's first Wales start since the 2024 Six Nations.
  • James Botham starts at number six, in place of Alex Mann. Botham came off the bench against Japan.
  • Adam Beard comes into the second row at number five, where Ben Carter started against Japan. Carter moves to the bench.
  • Gabriel Hamer-Webb starts on the wing, in place of Josh Adams. It is his first appearance in the Nations Championship.

Adams scored two tries against Japan, and his omission is the talking point of the selection. No reason has been given for the decision.

Bench and squad news

Gareth Thomas, Dillon Lewis and Taine Plumtree are in line for their first appearances of the campaign off the bench. Ben Carter also moves from the starting XV to the replacements. Tomas Francis, who was on the bench against Japan, has been released from the squad after a knee injury in training, and Keiron Assiratti has been called into the wider squad.

What Tandy said

Tandy was clear that the win over Japan was not enough: “It was good to get the win last Saturday, but some elements of our execution weren't where we wanted.” Wales lost the ball on their own lineout throw five times in that game, according to Reuters' match report.

Looking ahead, he said: “We have to go up another level this weekend and be more ruthless.” On Basham, he said: “I'm delighted that Taine gets an opportunity this weekend.”
