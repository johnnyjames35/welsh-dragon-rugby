Alex Callender: Age, Club, 50 Caps and Wales Captaincy

From netball to rugby

Alex Callender came to rugby late. She played netball for Wales at under-21 level and only took up rugby in 2017, after school friends invited her to a training session. Two years later she won her first Wales cap, against France in the 2019 Six Nations.

Sevens and Harlequins

She also captained Wales sevens in the 2021 Rugby Europe Sevens Championship Series. In 2024 she joined Harlequins in Premiership Women's Rugby.

Captain and comeback

Callender co-captained Wales with Kate Williams on the 2025 summer tour of Australia, which included a historic first win there, and at the 2025 World Cup. Ankle surgery after the World Cup and another injury on club duty ruled her out of the 2026 Six Nations, but she returned this autumn as co-captain.

Fifty caps

Callender won her 50th cap in the 17-0 win over Japan in Tokyo on 24 October 2026. A week later in Kumagaya, her turnover on her own line stopped Japan scoring a second try as Wales held on with 13 players.
