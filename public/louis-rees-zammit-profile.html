Louis Rees-Zammit: Age, Height, Club and Wales Career

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 5 November 2026 | ⏱ 3 min read

Louis Rees-Zammit is a 25-year-old Wales wing from Penarth who plays his club rugby for Bristol Bears. He is named on the wing for Wales against Japan on Saturday 7 November 2026, back in the Wales team after spending about 18 months chasing a career in the NFL.

From Wales debut to the NFL

Rees-Zammit won his first Wales cap as a replacement against France in October 2020 and scored his first Test try a month later against Georgia. In 2021 he was picked for the Lions tour of South Africa and scored three tries in four appearances.

In March 2024 he left rugby to chase a career in American football, signing with the Kansas City Chiefs as a running back. He did not make the Chiefs' final roster in August 2024 and moved to the Jacksonville Jaguars the same day, first on the practice squad and then, from February 2025, on the active roster. He ran the 40-yard dash in 4.43 seconds at a pro day.

Back in rugby with Bristol

He announced his return to rugby union on 31 July 2025 and joined Bristol Bears in August 2025.

His return has not been a straight line. Speaking in July, he said he had “taken a bit of time to find my feet again”, and Nation.Cymru reported that he had gone seven internationals without a try at that point. In the 2026 Six Nations Wales played him at full-back for all five games, although he has made clear he prefers the wing. He was moved back to the wing during the summer.

Rees-Zammit v Japan

Steve Tandy has named him on the wing for Saturday, alongside Josh Adams on the other flank. Ruck calls him “a massive threat with ball in hand” and says his Six Nations was held back by a backline that was not using his speed on the outside. Kick-off is at 4:40pm, live on ITV and S4C: see how to watch Wales v Japan, our full team announcement and the match preview.
