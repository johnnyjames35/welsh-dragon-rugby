England v Australia Preview: Kick-Off Time, TV and Team News

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 8 November 2026 | ⏱ 4 min read

England host Australia at Allianz Stadium, Twickenham, on Sunday 8 November. Kick-off is at 3:10pm UK time and the match is live on ITV1, with coverage starting at 2pm.

It is England’s first home game of the Nations Championship, and the first of their three Tests before Finals Weekend at Twickenham on 27 to 29 November. For Welsh fans it is also an early look at the Australia side that Wales face at the Principality Stadium on Saturday 21 November.

What is at stake

Both sides sit third in their hemisphere’s Nations Championship table. England have won two games and lost one, to South Africa in the summer. Australia have won once, against Italy, and lost to France and Ireland. At Finals Weekend the top team in each hemisphere plays the Grand Final and the other sides play placement matches, so every point counts.

The two sides also play for the Ella Mobbs Trophy, which England hold after a 25-7 win in November 2025.

England team news

Maro Itoje returns as captain after missing the summer Tests, with Jamie George, Ellis Genge and Ollie Chessum named as vice-captains. Fin Smith starts at fly-half after recovering from a calf problem, and George Furbank is at full-back after his summer was cut short by appendicitis. Ben Curry starts at openside flanker, his first Test since July 2025.

There are four changes from the side that started against Argentina in July: Furbank replaces Marcus Smith, Fin Baxter replaces Genge, Itoje replaces George Martin and Curry replaces Guy Pepper. Marcus Smith is among the replacements, and the uncapped Greg Fisilau is on the bench.

Australia team news

Les Kiss is Australia’s head coach, and Harry Wilson captains the side from number eight. Len Ikitau returns at centre after missing the 43-21 defeat by New Zealand. Declan Meredith starts at fly-half, with Carter Gordon left out of the 23, and Joseph-Aukuso Suaalii moves to the left wing so that Test rookie Isaac Henry can play at outside centre. The bench has plenty of power, with Taniela Tupou, Rob Valetini and Carlo Tizzano all among the replacements.

England have won three of the last five meetings. The 2024 game at Twickenham was decided when Max Jorgensen scored after the final siren.

What to watch for

Fin Smith’s return. England’s backline is almost the same as the one that beat Argentina in July, and Smith is the player who steers it.

The Australian bench. Wilson has said he is backing the replacements to make the difference, and Valetini and Tupou can change a game in the last half hour.

The mood in the England camp. Reports of unrest at their Pennyhill Park base have shadowed the build-up, although coach Steve Borthwick’s contract was recently extended through the 2028 Six Nations. A home win would answer a lot of questions.

For Wales, the useful ones to study are Meredith at fly-half and the pair of Suaalii and Ikitau in midfield, because Wales meet this team in Cardiff in two weeks.

How to watch

In the UK the game is live on ITV1 from 2pm, and you can stream it free on ITVX with a registration. In Ireland it is on Virgin Media, in France on TF1 and in Australia on Stan Sport.

Wales’ own games follow: Wales v New Zealand on Saturday 14 November at 2:10pm and Wales v Australia on Saturday 21 November at 8:10pm, both at the Principality Stadium and live on ITV. Read how Wales got on in our Wales v Japan report.
