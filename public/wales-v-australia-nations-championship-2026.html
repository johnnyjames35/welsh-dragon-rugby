Wales 26-29 Australia: Four Tries and a Fightback Fall Three Points Short in Cardiff

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 22 November 2026 | ⏱ 4 min read

Australia beat Wales 29-26 at the Principality Stadium on Saturday night, ending Wales’ hopes of a third straight win a week after they beat New Zealand for the first time since 1953. Wales came from 12 points down to lead 21-19 in the second half, and replacement hooker Ryan Elias scored a late try to cut the gap to three, but a 74th-minute penalty from Wallabies fly-half Carter Gordon proved the difference. It ended a run of four straight defeats for Australia.

What happens next. Wales finish fifth of six in the northern hemisphere table, which means a Finals Weekend game against Japan at Twickenham on Saturday 28 November (1.10pm). Australia face Ireland the night before. Our Round 6 round-up covers the other five games.

First half: Wales pressure, Australia points

Steve Tandy made four changes from the side that beat New Zealand, reverting to the team that started against Japan. Flanker James Botham, Player of the Match a week earlier, was the notable absentee, and Dewi Lake captained from hooker. It was also an 8.10pm kick-off, which BBC Sport reported was the latest start for a Wales home rugby match at the Principality Stadium.

Wales put plenty of pressure on Australia in the first half, but the Wallabies soaked it up and scored twice through their front row: first Taniela Tupou, then Brandon Paenga-Amosa, who made it 12-0. Wales got a score back before the break, when Lake went over and Sam Costelow converted, so it was 12-7 at half-time.

Second half: Wales lead, then Gordon’s boot

Australia attacked well after the restart, and Max Jorgensen scored early in the half to make it 19-7. Wales had been 12 points down before, and this time they answered at once. Lake was driven over for his second try in the 50th minute, and five minutes later Blair Murray scored a try that BBC Sport described as magical. Costelow converted both, and Wales led 21-19 for the first time in the match.

Australia had the answer. Len Ikitau scored in the 64th minute and Gordon converted to make it 26-21, and Gordon’s penalty in the 74th minute stretched the lead to 29-21. Elias scored in the 79th minute to bring Wales within three points, and Costelow was off target with the conversion, his first miss from the tee in a Wales shirt this year, according to AFP. Wales needed another try to win, and their last attack ended when the ball was knocked on.

What it means for Wales

Wales had won two games in a row, against Japan (28-21) and New Zealand (35-28), and three in a row would have been their first such run since the 2023 World Cup. Tandy told BBC Sport the main feeling was frustration at losing and at the chances Wales missed.

Wales finish the Nations Championship with three wins and three defeats, and by our count 149 points scored and 180 conceded.
