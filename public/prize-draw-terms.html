Prize Draw Terms

Win a £20 Amazon voucher: free entry, UK 18+, closes 21 November 2026.

Last updated: 26 October 2026

In short: join the Welsh Dragon Rugby match-day email list, free, before the end of Saturday 21 November 2026 and you are entered into a draw to win a £20 Amazon.co.uk voucher. UK residents aged 18 or over only. No purchase is needed.

1. Who is running the draw

The promoter is Big Bulldog UK Ltd, trading as Welsh Dragon Rugby (company number 15946705), registered office: 35 Heol Gleien, Lower Cwmtwrch, Swansea, SA9 2TW. You can contact us through our contact page.

2. The prize

One Amazon.co.uk gift voucher worth £20, sent to the winner by email. There is no cash alternative. If we cannot supply the voucher, we will give a prize of equal or greater value, such as a book token. One winner only.

3. Who can enter

  • You must live in the United Kingdom and be aged 18 or over.
  • The promoter, its directors and staff, and their immediate families cannot enter.
  • One entry per person. Using more than one email address to enter more than once is not allowed, and any extra entries will be removed.

4. How to enter

Entry is free. No purchase is needed. To enter:

  • Sign up for the Welsh Dragon Rugby match-day emails on our website using your email address.
  • Click the link in the confirmation email we send you. Your entry is only counted once you have confirmed.

People who confirmed their sign-up before the draw opened are entered automatically. The draw opens on 26 October 2026 and closes at 23:59 on Saturday 21 November 2026 (UK time). Entries confirmed after that time cannot be counted.

By signing up you also agree to receive our match-day emails. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in every email, and unsubscribing does not remove you from the draw.

5. The draw and the winner

  • The winner will be picked at random from all valid entries on Monday 23 November 2026.
  • We will email the winner within 7 days of the draw. If we have not heard back within 14 days of emailing, we may draw another winner.
  • The voucher will be emailed to the winner within 30 days of the closing date.
  • To find out who won, email us through the contact page within one month of the draw and ask. We will only give the winner's first name and county.

6. Your information

We use your email address to run the draw, to contact the winner and to send the voucher, and to send the match-day emails you signed up for. We do not sell your details. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.

7. General

  • The promoter's decision on all matters to do with the draw is final.
  • We are not responsible for entries that are late, lost or not received because of problems with the internet or email.
  • We may cancel or change the draw if something outside our control makes that necessary, and we will post any change on this page.
  • This draw is not sponsored, endorsed or run by Amazon, the Welsh Rugby Union or any rugby club.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of England and Wales.
