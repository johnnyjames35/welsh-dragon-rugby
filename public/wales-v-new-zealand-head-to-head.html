Wales v New Zealand: The Full Head-to-Head Record Before 14 November

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 16 October 2026 | ⏱ 5 min read

Every time the All Blacks come to Cardiff, the same number comes up: 1953. That was the last time Wales beat New Zealand, and more than seven decades later it is still the question every Welsh fan asks before kick-off.

Wales and New Zealand meet again on Saturday 14 November 2026 at the Principality Stadium. Here is the full head-to-head record, the three famous Welsh wins, the near misses, and what the recent results tell us.

The record at a glance

PlayedNZ winsWales winsDrawsPoints
383530NZ 1,271, Wales 456

New Zealand have won the last 34 meetings in a row, a run that started in December 1963. Wales have never beaten the All Blacks in New Zealand, losing all ten Tests played there, and New Zealand have also won all four meetings at Rugby World Cups.

The three Welsh wins

DateResultVenue
16 December 1905WON Wales 3, New Zealand 0Cardiff Arms Park
21 December 1935WON Wales 13, New Zealand 12National Stadium, Cardiff
19 December 1953WON Wales 13, New Zealand 8National Stadium, Cardiff

The 1905 match was the first Test between the two nations, during the Original All Blacks tour, and it is still talked about because New Zealand wing Bob Deans claimed he had scored a try that would have levelled the game. It was not given. In between the Welsh wins, the 1924 "Invincibles" beat Wales 19-0 at St Helen's in Swansea.

All three Welsh wins came in Cardiff, and all three in December. Wales won three of the first four meetings between the sides. Since then, it has been one-way traffic.

The closest calls since 1953

The streak has not always been comfortable for New Zealand. These are the tightest finishes, all in Cardiff:

The 2004 game is the one that still hurts: beaten by a single point. Wales also scored their highest total against New Zealand at the 2003 Rugby World Cup in Sydney, putting 37 points on the All Blacks, but still lost 53-37.

The heavy defeats

The other side of the record is tough reading. New Zealand's biggest win over Wales was 55-3 in Hamilton in June 2003, a 52-point margin. In Cardiff, the biggest margins are 38 points, in 2005 (3-41) and 2021 (16-54), and the All Blacks' highest score in Wales came in 2022, when they won 55-23.

New Zealand have scored more than 50 points in each of their last three visits to Cardiff. But last November did offer some hope. Wales were only 24-21 down around the 50-minute mark, Tom Rogers scored a hat-trick, and Louis Rees-Zammit added a late try. Wales' 26 points was their highest ever home score against the All Blacks.

Nobody in Wales needs reminding of the odds. But every streak ends eventually, and one day a Welsh side will be the one that finally does it.
