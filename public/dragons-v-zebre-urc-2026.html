Dragons 35-14 Zebre: Rodney Parade Win Built on a Fast Start

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 1 November 2026 | ⏱ 2 min read

The Dragons beat Zebre Parma 35-14 at Rodney Parade on Saturday 31 October, building a 14-0 half-time lead and then holding off the Italians after the break. The official attendance was 4,358.

The result

TeamScoreScorers
Dragons35 WINTries: David Richards, Anzelo Tuitavuki, Fine Inisi, Cai Evans, penalty try. Conversions: Tinus de Beer and others
Zebre Parma14Tries: Matteo Canali, Tobias Wade. Conversions: Giacomo da Re 2

Half-time: Dragons 14-0 Zebre. Venue: Rodney Parade, Newport. Competition: United Rugby Championship, Round 5. Yellow card: Simone Gesi (Zebre).

A strong first half

David Richards scored first, in the sixth minute, and Anzelo Tuitavuki added a second inside the first half hour. Tinus de Beer kicked the first of the Dragons’ conversions.

Zebre down to 14

Zebre were reduced to 14 men when Simone Gesi was sent to the sin-bin in the 55th minute. The Dragons used the extra man, with Fine Inisi scoring shortly after the card. Cai Evans also crossed, and a penalty try completed the Dragons’ scoring.

Zebre did score twice, through Matteo Canali and Tobias Wade, with Giacomo da Re converting both, so the visitors’ 14 points came from two tries and two conversions.
