Sisilia Tuipulotu: Age, Club, Family and Wales Career

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 7 November 2026 | ⏱ 4 min read

At a glance

Born14 August 2003
PositionProp
ClubHarlequins (from the 2026-27 season)
Previous clubGloucester-Hartpury
Wales caps33 going into the final game of the 2026 Six Nations, with more added this autumn

A powerhouse prop

Sisilia Tuipulotu is one of the most exciting forwards in Welsh rugby. She burst onto the scene as a teenager, and by 2023 the WRU was pointing out that their teenage prop was the team's top try scorer, with back-to-back player of the match awards.

A rugby family

Rugby runs right through the family. Her father, Sione Tu'ipulotu, won 29 caps for Tonga and played in Wales for Caerphilly and the Dragons. Her cousins include Wales and Lions legend Taulupe Faletau and Carwyn Tuipulotu, and her brother Kepu played for England at the World Under-20 Championship.

Injury and comeback

Tuipulotu missed the 2025 Six Nations after hamstring surgery. She returned for the summer series in Australia and went on to play at the 2025 World Cup, her second.

2026: club move and the WXV

After winning three Premiership Women's Rugby titles in a row with Gloucester-Hartpury, she signed for Harlequins for the 2026-27 season. She started the autumn with two tries in the 50-19 win over South Africa and scored again in the 29-5 win in Kumagaya. RugbyPass credits her with four tries across the WXV Global Series.

The Lions dream

The first British and Irish Lions women's tour takes place in 2027, and Tuipulotu has made no secret of wanting to be on it. Speaking to RugbyPass after the South Africa win, she said it is probably "number one on my goal list right now".

Quick answers

How old is Sisilia Tuipulotu?

Sisilia Tuipulotu was born on 14 August 2003, so she is 23.

What club does Sisilia Tuipulotu play for?

She plays for Harlequins, having joined from Gloucester-Hartpury for the 2026-27 season.

Is Sisilia Tuipulotu related to Taulupe Faletau?

Yes. The WRU has described Taulupe Faletau as her cousin. Her father, Sione Tu'ipulotu, played for Tonga.
