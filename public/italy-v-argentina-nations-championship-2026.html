Italy 14-19 Argentina: Pumas Survive a Late Scare in Genoa as Winless Italy Fall Again

✎ By John James | 📅 14 November 2026 | ⏱ 4 min read

Argentina beat Italy 19-14 at the Stadio Luigi Ferraris in Genoa on Saturday 14 November, in Round 5 of the Nations Championship. It was a scrappy, error-filled match that left the Azzurri with a fifth defeat in five games and handed the Pumas their tenth win in a row over Italy. Argentina picked up only their second victory of the competition, and their first since July, to move into third place in the southern hemisphere table on 11 points, three clear of Australia.

Who scored

TeamScorers
Italy (14)Tries: Monty Ioane, penalty try. Conversion: Tommaso Allan
Argentina (19)Tries: Bautista Bernasconi, Rodrigo Isgró, Mateo Carreras. Conversions: Tomás Albornoz (2)

It was 7-7 at half-time. The reports we checked differ on some minutes, so we have kept the timings loose.

First half: Italy strike first

Italy made the better start. Monty Ioane crossed early on, Allan converted, and the hosts led 7-0. Argentina then settled and shortly before half-time hooker Bernasconi finished a driving maul, with Albornoz adding the extras to level it at 7-7.

Second half: Pumas pull clear, Italy come back

Isgró put Argentina in front with a try in the 55th minute, although his conversion hit the upright. Carreras then extended the lead with a second Argentine try of the half, converted by Albornoz, to make it 19-7 with around 20 minutes left.

Discipline was the story of the closing stages. Argentina were shown three yellow cards over the match, and a penalty try for Italy cut the gap to 19-14 and set up a grandstand finish. The hosts pushed for a winning score but could not find it. Washington Post reported 32 penalties in the match, split evenly between the sides, with the Pumas enjoying most of the possession and territory but turning it over too often.

Italy coach Gonzalo Quesada said afterwards that his side had the chance to win it and let it slip away.

What it means

Italy have lost all five of their Nations Championship matches and will finish bottom of the northern hemisphere group. They did take a first losing bonus point of the competition. They host Fiji in Udine on 21 November, and could meet Fiji again the following weekend in the wooden-spoon match at the finals at Twickenham.

Argentina travel to Paris next to face France, who beat South Africa 21-19 on Friday: see our France v South Africa report. Before this win, the Pumas’ only victory in the competition had come at home to Wales in July.
