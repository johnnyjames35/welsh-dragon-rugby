Wales v Japan Preview: Kick-Off Time, TV and What to Expect

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 31 October 2026 | ⏱ 4 min read

Wales open their November Nations Championship games against Japan at the Principality Stadium on Saturday 7 November. Kick-off is at 4:40pm and the match is live on ITV and S4C.

It is a fixture Wales used to treat as routine. After the last two years it is not, so here is what is at stake, who is in the squad and what happened last time.

What is at stake

Wales lost two of their three Nations Championship games in July. They beat Fiji 39-24, lost 35-21 to Argentina and were beaten 43-0 by South Africa, which left them fifth in the Northern Hemisphere table on five points.

A win in the Championship earns four points, with a bonus point for scoring four tries and another for losing by seven or fewer. The leaders, France, were on 12 points after July, so Wales are well behind, but there are still up to 15 points available across the three November games. After those, the Finals Weekend at Twickenham runs from 27 to 29 November, where the top team in each hemisphere plays the Grand Final and the other sides play placement matches.

Japan sit fifth in the Southern Hemisphere table after one win and two losses in July, on four points. That makes this a meeting of two sides who both need to start November well.

Who are Japan?

Japan are coached by Eddie Jones. They beat Italy in July, lost to Ireland and France, and in September they beat Fiji 20-15 to win the 2026 Pacific Nations Cup. They played Fiji again on 24 October in a double-header marking the Japan Rugby Football Union's 100th anniversary, and after Wales they face England and Scotland in the Championship.

Their squad for the Wales game has not been confirmed as we publish this.

Wales squad news

Steve Tandy has a 39-player squad, with Dewi Lake as captain. The only change so far is on the wing: Dragons back Rio Dyer has been ruled out with a hamstring injury and the uncapped Scarlets 21-year-old Macs Page has been called up in his place. Page still has to complete the return-to-play head injury protocols before he can be cleared to play. He is one of four uncapped players in the group, alongside Rhys Barratt, Che Hope and Aneurin Owen.

The match-day 23 has not been named yet. We will update this page when it is. Read the full story in our report on the Dyer and Page change, or browse the Wales Nations Championship squad.

Japan's win in Kitakyushu was only their second ever against Wales. The Cardiff game in November was decided by Jarrod Evans' penalty with the last kick of the match, after Josh Adams' yellow card was upgraded to a 20-minute red and Wales failed to score while Japan had the extra men. Overall Wales have won 11 of their 13 Tests against Japan and have never lost to them in Cardiff. The full record is in our Wales v Japan head-to-head.

What to watch for

Discipline. Cards swung the match in Cardiff last year, and with bonus points on offer both sides need to keep 15 players on the pitch.

The debut watch. If Macs Page clears the head injury protocols and Wales pick him, he could win a first cap in front of a home crowd at a stadium that holds almost 74,000.

A fast start. Wales have to bank points early in November, and a try bonus point could matter when the table is this tight.

How to watch

The game is live on ITV and S4C in the UK, with kick-off at 4:40pm. The next two home games follow in successive weeks: Wales v New Zealand on 14 November and Wales v Australia on 21 November. Our autumn fixtures guide has every kick-off time in one place.
