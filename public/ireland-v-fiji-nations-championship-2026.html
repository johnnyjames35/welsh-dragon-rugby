Ireland 43-15 Fiji: Second-Half Surge Sees Off a Tough Fiji Test in Dublin

✎ By John James | 📅 14 November 2026 | ⏱ 4 min read

Ireland beat Fiji 43-15 at the Aviva Stadium in Dublin on Saturday 14 November, in Round 5 of the Nations Championship. It was far from comfortable. Fiji led briefly and Ireland trailed at one stage, but the hosts scored 26 unanswered points after the break and ran in seven tries to finish with a bonus-point win.

Who scored

TeamScorers
Ireland (43)Tries: Tommy O’Brien (2), Robert Baloucoune (2), Ronan Kelleher, Andrew Porter, Nick Timoney
Fiji (15)Tries: Josua Tuisova, Peceli Yato. Penalty: Ben Volavola

Half-time: Ireland 17-15 Fiji. The reports we checked do not agree on every minute, so we have kept the timings loose.

First half: Fiji hit back

O’Brien gave Ireland an eighth-minute lead, crossing wide on the left. Fiji then capitalised on Irish errors. Tuisova scored after a line-out maul, and Yato went over after a break by Jiuta Wainiqolo, putting the visitors in front. Baloucoune, left unmarked on both occasions, scored twice to put Ireland back ahead. Ireland captain Tadhg Beirne was then sin-binned, and Ben Volavola kicked a penalty that left it 17-15 to Ireland at the break.

Second half: Ireland pull away

Fiji ran out of steam. Kelleher scored early in the second half, Porter added another, and O’Brien got his second from a well-judged Sam Prendergast kick. Nick Timoney finished it off late with a try under the posts. Fiji replacement prop Inoke Ravuiwasa was shown a yellow card for a swinging arm on James McNabney, who was making his Ireland debut off the bench.

Andy Farrell made seven changes to the side for this game.

What it means

The win leaves Ireland level on 20 points with England, one point behind northern hemisphere leaders France. Next up is a showdown with world champions South Africa in Dublin on Saturday 21 November. Sky Sports reported that Ireland need to beat the Springboks and hope both France (against Argentina) and England (against New Zealand) lose to reach the final at Twickenham on 29 November. Fiji’s last pool game is against Italy in Udine on the same day.

The Springboks lost 21-19 to France on Friday night: see our France v South Africa report.
