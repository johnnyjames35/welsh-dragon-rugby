Sean Lynn is head coach, and back-rowers Alex Callender and Kate Williams are co-captains for the Japan tour. The 30-player squad includes Keira Bevan, Seren Lockwood, Courtney Keight and Sisilia Tuipulotu. You can see every name, with club and uncapped players, in our Japan tour squad guide.

Frequently asked questions

When do Wales Women next play?

Wales Women's next match is Wales v England on Saturday 10 April 2027, the opening round of the 2027 Women's Six Nations. Their last Test of 2026 was a 29-5 win over Japan at Kumagaya Rugby Stadium on 31 October, which completed a 2-0 series win.

When is the 2027 Women's Six Nations and who do Wales play?

The 2027 Women's Six Nations runs from 10 April to 15 May 2027. Wales play England (home, 10 April), Italy (away, 17 April), Ireland (home, 24 April), France (away, 8 May) and Scotland (away, 15 May).

Who is the Wales Women head coach?

Sean Lynn is head coach of Wales Women. Alex Callender and Kate Williams are the co-captains for the 2026 autumn tour of Japan.

How can I watch Wales Women?

The BBC and S4C are the UK broadcasters for the 2027 Women's Six Nations. The WXV Global Series Tests in Japan were listed on BBC iPlayer, but check the BBC Sport listings in the week of each match as channels can change.
