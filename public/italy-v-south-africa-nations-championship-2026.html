South Africa 66-7 Italy: Springboks Score Nine Tries in Turin

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 7 November 2026 | ⏱ 3 min read

South Africa beat Italy 66-7 at the Allianz Stadium in Turin on Saturday, scoring nine converted tries in Round 4 of the Nations Championship. The world champions were 45-0 up at half-time and the contest was effectively over long before the break. The win was their fourth in a row and keeps them top of the Southern Hemisphere table.

Italy try (1): Federico Ruzza, converted.

First half: six tries before the break

Fly-half Sacha Feinberg-Mngomezulu scored the first of the Springboks' tries, and the pack soon took over. Hooker Malcolm Marx scored from a driving maul after 20 minutes, and captain Siya Kolisi went over from another maul three minutes later. Marx added a second before the interval, alongside tries from scrum-half Morné van den Berg and full-back Damian Willemse. Six tries and a penalty made it 45-0 at half-time.

Second half: Italy find a reply

Prop Wilco Louw scored the first try of the second half from close range to take South Africa past 50. Italy, who were missing several players through injury, finally got on the scoreboard when Federico Ruzza finished a well-worked attack in the corner just after the hour mark. Paul de Villiers then scored from a maul, and wing Ethan Hooker finished a set-piece move on full-time for the ninth try.

What it means

South Africa now have the maximum 20 points from four games in the Southern Hemisphere table, with a points-difference lead of 89 over New Zealand. They face France next weekend. Last November the same fixture in Turin finished 32-14 to the Springboks, so this was a much heavier defeat for Italy.

For Welsh fans, this is the side that beat Wales 43-0 in July's Nations Championship. Wales open their own autumn today against Japan at the Principality Stadium (4:40pm): see our match preview and how to watch.
