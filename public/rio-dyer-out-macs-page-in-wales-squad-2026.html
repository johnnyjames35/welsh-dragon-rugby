Rio Dyer Out, Macs Page In: Wales Change Their Autumn Squad

Welsh Dragon Rugby | 📅 30 October 2026 | ⏱ 3 min read

Wales have made their first change to Steve Tandy's squad for the November Nations Championship Tests. Dragons back Rio Dyer has been released with a hamstring injury, and uncapped Scarlets 21-year-old Macs Page has been called up to replace him.

What happened to Rio Dyer

Dyer hurt his hamstring in the Dragons' 31-14 United Rugby Championship defeat at the Ospreys on Saturday 24 October, which we covered in our Ospreys v Dragons report. He had only just been recalled to the Wales squad after missing the Six Nations and the summer fixtures. He is now ruled out of the Tests against Japan, New Zealand and Australia.

Who is Macs Page?

Page is a 21-year-old back who broke through at the Scarlets as an outside centre and has been playing on the wing this season. He has not won a senior cap. He was part of Wales' 2025 tour party to Japan without playing, and he was named in the team of the tournament at the 2024 World Rugby U20 Championship. Before rugby took over he spent three years in Swansea City's football academy.

His form is the reason for the call. Reports put him on five tries in five games across all competitions, including braces against the Dragons and Newcastle, plus a try against the Stormers. You can read about two of those games in our reports on Scarlets 47-14 Newcastle and Stormers 52-40 Scarlets. Wales kicking coach Alex Davies described him as “an all-action player”.

One condition. Page took a knock to the head against the Stormers and has to complete the return-to-play head injury protocols before he can be cleared to play, so his involvement depends on that process.

What it changes

The squad stays at 39 players. Page becomes the fourth uncapped player in the group, alongside Cardiff prop Rhys Barratt and the Dragons pair Che Hope and Aneurin Owen. Dewi Lake remains captain.

Dyer was not in FloRugby's predicted XV, which had Josh Adams, Louis Rees-Zammit and Tom Rogers in the back three. That is one outlet's guess rather than an official team, but it suggests his loss is more about depth than a certain starter. The match-day 23 will be named closer to each game.
